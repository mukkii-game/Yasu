## Additional sound effects

The game author supplied the following royalty-free MP3 effects for this
project. The later batch arrived with its Japanese filenames stripped in
transit, so those are listed under the names the author used for them:

- `不安（ピアノ演奏）.mp3` — bundled as `public/audio/anxiety.mp3`
- `ビシッとツッコミ2.mp3` — bundled as `public/audio/punchline-hit.mp3`
- `ショック1.mp3` — bundled as `public/audio/reveal-shock.mp3`
- `ドーン.mp3` — bundled as `public/audio/final-boom.mp3`
- `間抜け7.mp3` — bundled as `public/audio/comic-pratfall.mp3`
- `マシンガンの弾倉を外す.mp3` — bundled as `public/audio/magazine-release.mp3`
- `革靴で歩く.mp3` — bundled as `public/audio/leather-steps.mp3`
- the supplied dissonant chord — bundled as `public/audio/dissonance.mp3`
- `シーン切り替え1.mp3` — bundled as `public/audio/scene-change.mp3`

`お寺の鐘.mp3` was supplied at the same time but is not used in the game.
`scene-change.mp3` is likewise unused: it was supplied as the alternative to
`comic-pratfall.mp3` for the boss route's opening laugh, and is kept bundled so
that choice can be changed in one word.
